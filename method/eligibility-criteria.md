# Eligibility criteria

## Included

Cases had to meet all four criteria.

1. Linked to an urban area
2. Community involvement
3. Ecological diversity
4. Documented, measurable benefits

## Scope

- English-language peer-reviewed and grey literature
- Published 2015 to 2024
- Treescape types: woodlands, rural and peri-urban forests, nature reserves, riparian zones, large public parks, urban wooded green spaces, restored habitats, and tree-covered catchments

## Excluded

- Treescapes used for timber production
- Ecosystem services not directly tied to community engagement or well-being (water purification and retention, carbon sequestration, coastal resilience, property value)
- Duplicates, inaccessible records, cases outside Europe, and cases without a substantial community-based treescape component

## Result

131 case studies were included.
