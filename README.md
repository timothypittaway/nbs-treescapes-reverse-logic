# NBS treescapes reverse-logic analysis

Companion repository for the article below. It brings together the structured data, search strategy and analytical framework reported in the paper, in a form that other researchers can reuse.

> Pittaway, T., Townsend, L., & Hardy, C. (2026). From Community Benefits to Vulnerabilities: Reverse-Logic Analysis of Nature-Based Solution Treescapes Across Europe. *International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health*, 23(6), 691. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph23060691

Open access full text: [PMC13299224](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13299224/)

## About the study

The study reviewed 131 European nature-based solution (NBS) treescape case studies drawn from 13 open-access NBS repositories. Community benefits were coded into eleven categories and mapped across European biogeographical regions.

The reverse-logic method then works backwards from the benefits a treescape delivers to the socio-ecological vulnerabilities those benefits imply. This gives planners a way to spot region-specific vulnerabilities before a project is designed.

## Notes on the data

All data here are taken from tables and text in the published article. They are summary data at the category level, not the case-level coding sheet.

Where the article reports a figure only as an image (the PRISMA flow numbers and the region by benefit breakdown), those values are not reproduced here. Cells the article does not report are left blank rather than estimated.

No personal or participant data are involved. All cases come from publicly available repositories.
